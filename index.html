System rules

• Each quest +10 XP, a full clear +20 bonus.
• A day is kept with at least 4 of 6 quests (two-thirds).
• Every finished day that isn't kept costs XP: −20, then −40, −60, −80 and up to −100 for each further missed day in a row. Your level and rank drop when your XP does.
• Keeping a day resets the penalty back to −20.
• Pause mode (travel, illness): no penalties or reminders, streaks are frozen, and that week's dungeon is sealed.
